About

I am Anandu, a video editor, cinematographer and colourist working between Bengaluru and Kochi.

I work closely with each client to understand their tone and intention first. Whether it is a brand film, a short story or a documentary, the mood is built for that project rather than pulled off a preset shelf.

Recent work includes campaigns for Lexus, Skoda, MakeMyTrip, Rare Rabbit and Sid’s Farm, and a reel that crossed 33 million views, around thirty times the creator’s previous best.

Story first, not just clean

I shape emotion and rhythm. Pacing, sound and tone serve the narrative before they serve the technique.

A defined colour identity

I build custom palettes and LUTs so each film ends up with a look of its own.

Both sides of the frame

Because I shoot and edit, I know what gets captured on set and how it has to live in the cut.

Disciplined, but not rigid

Colour-managed workflows in Resolve and Fusion underneath, an intuitive and organic process on top.
