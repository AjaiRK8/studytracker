Privacy Policy

CA Planner (CA Final study planner) · Last updated 12 November 2026

CA Planner is a free study planner for CA students. It runs entirely in your browser at ajairk8.github.io/studytracker. This page explains what the planner does with your data.

What the planner collects

If you sign in with Google, the planner receives your name and email address from Google. If you create a username and password instead, the planner stores that username and a hashed password on your device only.

Your study data (punch in/out times, chapters, notes, session logs, settings, and any images or voice notes you add) is created by you inside the planner.

Where your data is stored

  • In your own browser (local storage) on the device you use.
  • If you sign in with Google, in a private app folder in your own Google Drive. The planner requests only the Google Drive “app data” permission, so it can see only the files it created itself. It cannot see or change your other Drive files, Gmail or Photos.
  • In a backup or save file on your own computer, if you choose to link one.

The developer does not run a server or database for your study data and cannot see it. The only data the developer holds is the user list described below.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to run the planner for you: to show your dashboard and notes, and to sync them between your own devices. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any AI model.

Developer user list

When you sign in with Google, your name and Google email address are added to a private list kept by the developer, together with the dates you first and last opened the planner and a visit count. The developer uses this only to know how many people use the planner, to contact users about updates or problems, and to keep the planner running. It is stored in a private Google Sheet that only the developer can open, and it is not sold or shared.

To do this, the planner sends a temporary Google sign-in token to the developer’s Google Apps Script, which uses it only to confirm your email address and does not store it. No study data, notes, or files are sent. People who use a username and password instead of Google sign-in are not added to this list, because the planner does not collect an email address for them.

To have your entry deleted, email the address below and the developer will remove it. You can also stop future updates by signing out and revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your device or your Google Drive unless you choose to export or share it yourself, for example by creating a PDF report.

Deleting your data

  • Use “Delete account” in the planner’s Settings to remove the account and its data from your browser.
  • Delete the planner’s data from your Google Drive using the storage manager in Settings, or remove the app’s data from Google Drive settings.
  • Revoke the planner’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

The planner’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The planner is intended for exam students and is not directed at children under 13.
