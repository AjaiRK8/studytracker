Privacy Policy

CA Final Study Planner · Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what the CA Final Study Planner (“the planner”, at ajairk8.github.io/studytracker) does with your information. The planner is a free personal study tool made by Ajai R Krishnan. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by ICAI.

1. What the planner stores

  • Study data you enter: punch-in/out sessions, chapter progress, sticky notes (including Last Day Revision question numbers, page numbers and summaries), and your settings such as exam date and daily target.
  • Account details: if you sign in with Google, your Gmail address and display name. If you create a username-and-password account, your username and a salted hash of your password (the password itself is never stored).

2. Where your data is kept

  • In your browser: your data is saved in your browser’s local storage on the device you use.
  • In your own Google Drive (Google sign-in only): your data is also saved as a file named ca-final-planner.json in a hidden application-data folder of your own Google Drive, so it can sync between your devices. Only this planner, signed in as you, can read that folder. It does not appear in your normal Drive file list.
  • No developer servers: the planner has no server or database of its own. The developer cannot see, access or recover your data.

3. Google user data

When you choose “Sign in with Google”, the planner asks for these permissions:

  • Email address and basic profile (name): used only to identify your account inside the planner and show it on screen.
  • Google Drive application-data folder (drive.appdata): used only to save and load the planner’s own data file described above. The planner cannot see, list or modify any of your other Drive files.

The planner’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties, and not read by humans. It is used only to provide the sync feature you asked for.

4. Voice typing

If you use the microphone button, speech-to-text is performed by your browser’s built-in speech recognition. Depending on your browser (for example Chrome), your audio may be processed by the browser vendor’s speech service under its own privacy policy. The planner itself does not record or store audio, only the text that appears in the box.

5. Sharing

Your data is not shared with anyone. The planner contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. Loading Google sign-in contacts Google’s servers, which is governed by Google’s own privacy policy.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Export: use “Export backup” or the CSV exports in Settings to keep your own copy.
  • Remove from this browser: use “Delete account” in Settings, or clear this site’s data in your browser.
  • Revoke Google access: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose the planner, and remove access.
  • Delete the Drive copy: in Google Drive go to Settings → Manage apps, find the planner, and choose “Delete hidden app data”.

7. Security

Data travels between your browser and Google over HTTPS. The Google access token is kept only in the page’s memory and is not saved. Your saved data is protected by your device and Google account security and is not separately encrypted by the planner, so please avoid putting highly confidential information in notes. Passwords for username accounts cannot be recovered if forgotten.

8. Children

The planner is intended for exam students and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
